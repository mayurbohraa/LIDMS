GETTING STARTED

Your lab workflow, explained.

A few answers to help you get moving.

How do I get an account?

Request access on the registration page. Your administrator approves the account and sets your role. Use your username and password to sign in.

How do stock requests work?

Choose an item, quantity and purpose. An administrator reviews the request. Approval deducts the issued units from available stock. Requests cannot be approved twice, and insufficient stock prevents approval.

How do I report and resolve an issue?

Open Complaints and report an issue. An administrator assigns a technician. The assigned technician can update its status and must record a resolution before closing it. Faculty can track their own reported issues.

Who can change inventory?

Administrators and technicians can maintain equipment records and record stock adjustments with a reason. Archiving an item preserves its request and movement history.

What appears in my reports?

Inventory is shared across the workspace. Administrators can export all requests and complaints. Other accounts export their own requests and complaints they are permitted to view.

I cannot sign in. What should I do?

Check that your account has been approved. If you forgot your password, contact your lab administrator. Repeated failed login attempts temporarily block that account and connection combination for 15 minutes.
