FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How do I get an account?+

Your lab administrator creates or approves your account and assigns the appropriate role. Then you can log in with your email and password.

Can I request equipment that is not in stock?+

Yes. Use Post Stock Request to describe the equipment you need and why. Your administrator reviews the request. Approval of a new equipment proposal does not issue existing stock.

Where can I track my requests and complaints?+

Your dashboard shows relevant requests and complaints. Open a record for its details, or check Your Updates for decisions, assignments and status changes.

Who records equipment returns?+

An administrator or technician records the returned quantity and condition. The stock balance and your issued-equipment history update together.

Will everyone see the same records?+

No. Administrators oversee lab records. Faculty see their own requests, loans and complaints. Technicians see maintenance assigned to them or reported by them, and manage stock and returns.
